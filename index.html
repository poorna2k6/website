Meet Your Photographer

Passionate About Every Frame

Welcome to PPC PIX — where every photograph tells a story and every moment becomes a lasting treasure. Based in the heart of Dallas-Fort Worth, I've had the privilege of serving the vibrant South Indian and Telugu community across DFW for over five years.

From the sacred rituals of a Gruhapravesham to the joyful chaos of a cake smash, from the serene beauty of a maternity shoot to the grandeur of a cultural festival — I approach every session with the same devotion: capturing your authentic moments with an artistic eye and a warm heart.

With cinematic videography and FAA-certified drone capabilities, I deliver a complete visual storytelling experience. Every gallery is presented privately through SmugMug — beautifully organized and accessible for a lifetime of revisiting your cherished memories.
